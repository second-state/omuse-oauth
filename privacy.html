Privacy Policy

OMuse, by Second State · Effective 6 November 2026

In short: OMuse runs on your own server and keeps your data there. Second State keeps only the email address you registered with. We do not collect, store, sell or use the content of your email, calendar, files, chats or any other data your OMuse handles, and we never receive your Google data or tokens.

1. Who we are and what this covers

This policy is from Second State ("we", "us") and covers OMuse, the personal AI assistant, and the web pages at second-state.github.io/omuse-oauth/ that OMuse uses for Google sign-in. OMuse is open-source software (github.com/second-state/omuse). Each OMuse installation runs on a server dedicated to one user — a computer you run yourself, or a server set up for you alone ("your server").

2. What Second State keeps: your registration email only

When you register for OMuse, we keep the email address you register with. We use it only to provide your OMuse service: to identify your account, to send you service messages (setup, security, billing and changes to these terms) and to answer you when you write to us. If you pay for OMuse, the payment is handled by our payment processor (Stripe); we do not store your card number.

We keep no other customer data. We do not retain or use the content of your messages, your email, your calendar, your files, your profile, your memory or anything else your OMuse server handles.

3. Where your data is: your own server

Everything OMuse works with — your chats, your profile and memory, the files you upload, the reports it makes, and the access tokens of the accounts you connect — is stored on your server. Passwords, tokens and other secrets are kept encrypted in that server's vault. The AI agent on the server never sees your tokens; it asks the server's security layer (Sentinel) to act, and the security layer asks your approval for anything that sends, posts, pays or deletes.

4. Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar or Google Drive, OMuse requests these permissions:

  • openid and email: your Google account's email address, to show which account is connected.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: to read your events, your calendar's time zone and free/busy times, and to add, change or cancel events when you ask OMuse to (changes and invitations ask for your approval first).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: only the files you pick in OMuse's Google Drive picker and the files OMuse creates in your Drive. OMuse cannot see any other file in your Drive.

How it is accessed and stored. When you approve access on Google's page, Google sends a one-time code to our sign-in relay page, which runs in your browser, asks you to confirm your server's address and passes the code to that server. The relay page stores nothing. Your server exchanges the code for tokens directly with Google and keeps them encrypted in its vault. Your Google data is read by your server, when you ask for something that needs it, and is not sent to Second State.

How it is used. Only to provide the features you use: answering your questions about your schedule, finding free time, preparing your daily plan ("What's next"), adding or changing events you ask for, and working with the Drive files you pick or that OMuse makes for you. To answer you, your server may send the relevant parts of that data (for example today's events) to the AI model provider it is set up with — see section 5.

What we never do. We do not sell Google user data; we do not use it for advertising; we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models; and no person reads it, except where you ask us to help with your own server and give your consent, where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

OMuse's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Services your server works with

To do what you ask, your server sends data to the services it is connected to — chosen by you, or set up for you as part of your server — and their own terms and privacy policies apply:

  • The AI model provider your server uses (for example an OpenAI-compatible API): the request and the data needed to answer it.
  • The accounts you connect: your mailbox (IMAP/SMTP), Google Calendar and Drive, Notion, Slack, Telegram and others.
  • Search and travel data services (for example web search, flight and hotel search) and the websites OMuse opens for you, such as an airline's or a government's form.

Second State does not receive this data.

6. Your choices and deleting your data

  • Disconnect an account in OMuse under Connections: the account's tokens are deleted from your server.
  • Revoke OMuse's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete your data on your server: in OMuse (chats, memory, files, the vault) or by deleting the server's data.
  • Delete your registration email with us by asking us (section 9). We delete it, except where the law requires us to keep billing records.

7. Security

Secrets on your server are encrypted at rest; a Google sign-in carries a one-time state that your server checks, the sign-in through the relay page also uses PKCE, and the relay page asks you to confirm where your sign-in goes. No system is perfectly secure; please keep your server's password private and your server up to date.

8. Children

OMuse is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly register them.

9. Contact and changes

Questions or requests, including deleting your registration email: write to support@secondstate.io, or open an issue at github.com/second-state/omuse/issues. If we change this policy, we post the new version here with its effective date and, for important changes, tell you by email.
