OMuse — your personal AI assistant

By Second State

OMuse is a personal AI assistant that runs on your own server — a computer you run, or a server set up for you alone. It works with the accounts you connect: it reads and drafts email, looks at your calendar and finds free time, plans and books travel, fills in travel forms, works with the files you pick from Google Drive, researches topics and writes reports. Anything that sends, posts, pays or deletes waits for your approval.

How OMuse uses your Google account

When you click Connect on the Google Calendar or Google Drive card in OMuse and approve access on Google's page, your OMuse server receives a token from Google and keeps it encrypted on that server. It uses it for:

  • Google Calendar (calendar.events, calendar.readonly): to show your events, find free time across your calendars, prepare your daily plan ("What's next"), and add or change events when you ask it to — changes and invitations ask for your approval first.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): only the files you pick in OMuse's Google Drive picker and the files OMuse creates for you (for example a report or an edited video it saves to your Drive). OMuse cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • Your email address (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.

Second State does not receive your Google data or your tokens. See the privacy policy for the details, and the terms of service.
